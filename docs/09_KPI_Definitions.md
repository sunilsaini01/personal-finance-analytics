# KPI Definitions

# Card Transaction & Merchant Analytics

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used throughout the Personal Finance Analytics & Budget Intelligence System.

The objective is to provide standardized business definitions so that analysts, developers, and stakeholders interpret every metric consistently.

---

# KPI Summary

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Revenue | Total value of all positive transactions |
| Total Transactions | Total number of completed transactions |
| Average Transaction Value | Average value per transaction |
| Monthly Revenue | Revenue generated each month |
| Yearly Revenue | Revenue generated each year |
| Revenue Growth | Month-over-Month or Year-over-Year revenue change |
| Customer Spending | Total spending by a customer |
| Merchant Revenue | Revenue generated by a merchant |
| Category Revenue | Revenue generated by an MCC category |
| Payment Method Revenue | Revenue by payment channel |
| Net Spend | Total spend minus refunds over a period |
| Rolling Average | Smoothed monthly revenue trend |

---

# 1. Total Revenue

## Business Definition

The total monetary value generated from all successful transactions.

## Formula

```
SUM(amount)
```

## Business Importance

- Overall business performance
- Executive reporting
- Revenue monitoring

---

# 2. Total Transactions

## Business Definition

Total number of completed financial transactions.

## Formula

```
COUNT(transaction_id)
```

## Business Importance

- Customer activity
- Platform usage
- Merchant performance

---

# 3. Average Transaction Value

## Business Definition

Average amount spent in a single transaction.

## Formula

```
SUM(amount) / COUNT(transaction_id)
```

## Business Importance

- Spending behavior
- Customer purchasing power
- Merchant benchmarking

---

# 4. Monthly Revenue

## Business Definition

Revenue generated during a calendar month.

## Formula

```
SUM(amount)
GROUP BY Month
```

## Business Importance

- Trend analysis
- Financial planning
- Monthly reporting

---

# 5. Yearly Revenue

## Business Definition

Total revenue generated during a calendar year.

## Formula

```
SUM(amount)
GROUP BY Year
```

## Business Importance

- Business growth
- Executive reporting
- Strategic planning

---

# 6. Revenue Growth

## Business Definition

Percentage increase or decrease in revenue compared with the previous period.

## Formula

```
(Current Revenue - Previous Revenue)
/ Previous Revenue × 100
```

## Business Importance

- Growth measurement
- Performance monitoring
- Forecasting

---

# 7. Customer Spending

## Business Definition

Total amount spent by an individual customer.

## Formula

```
SUM(amount)
GROUP BY client_id
```

## Business Importance

- Customer segmentation
- Loyalty programs
- Customer Lifetime Value analysis

---

# 8. Merchant Revenue

## Business Definition

Revenue generated by each merchant.

## Formula

```
SUM(amount)
GROUP BY merchant_id
```

## Business Importance

- Merchant ranking
- Partnership decisions
- Revenue concentration analysis

---

# 9. Category Revenue

## Business Definition

Revenue generated within each Merchant Category Code (MCC).

## Formula

```
SUM(amount)
GROUP BY mcc
```

## Business Importance

- Spending analysis
- Category performance
- Business planning

---

# 10. Payment Method Revenue

## Business Definition

Revenue generated by each payment method.

## Payment Types

- Swipe Transaction
- Chip Transaction
- Online Transaction

## Formula

```
SUM(amount)
GROUP BY use_chip
```

## Business Importance

- Payment preference analysis
- Digital adoption tracking
- Payment strategy

---

# 11. Net Spend

## Business Definition

Net card spend over a reporting period (total spend minus refunds).
This dataset is credit-card transaction data with no salary/income
stream, so "Income − Expenses" does not apply here — see
04_savings_analysis.sql for the spend/refund breakdown this KPI is
built from.

## Formula

```
Total Spend − Total Refunds
```

## Business Importance

- Financial stability
- Budget planning
- Liquidity monitoring

---

# 12. Rolling Average

## Business Definition

Average revenue over a moving time window (e.g., 3 months).

## Formula

```
AVG(revenue)
OVER(...)
```

## Business Importance

- Trend smoothing
- Forecasting
- Seasonality analysis

# KPI Usage in Dashboard

The following KPIs will be displayed in the Power BI dashboard.

| Dashboard Page | KPIs |
|----------------|------|
| Executive Overview | Total Revenue, Total Transactions, Net Spend |
| Customer Analysis | Customer Spending, Average Transaction |
| Merchant Analysis | Merchant Revenue, Transactions |
| Category Analysis | Category Revenue, Contribution % |
| Payment Analysis | Revenue by Payment Method |
| Spend Analysis | Net Spend, Rolling Average |
| Time Series Analysis | Monthly Revenue, Growth %, Running Total |

# KPI Best Practices

- Use consistent calculation logic across SQL and Power BI.
- Clearly define aggregation levels.
- Document every KPI before dashboard development.
- Validate KPI values against SQL query outputs.
- Avoid duplicate metric definitions.

---

# Conclusion

The KPIs defined in this document provide a consistent measurement framework for financial reporting, business analysis, and dashboard development. Standardized KPI definitions ensure reliable decision-making and maintain consistency across SQL reports and Power BI dashboards.
