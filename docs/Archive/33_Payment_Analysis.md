# Payment Analysis

## Business Problem

Customers use multiple payment methods while making purchases. Understanding which payment methods are used most frequently, which generate the highest transaction value, and how usage changes over time helps financial institutions optimize payment infrastructure, improve fraud monitoring, and design better customer rewards programs.

This analysis answers questions such as:

- Which payment method is used most frequently?
- Which payment method generates the highest revenue?
- How has payment method usage changed over time?
- Which payment method has the highest average transaction value?
- Which payment methods contribute most to total revenue?
- What are the highest-value transactions by payment method?

---

# Dataset Used

**Primary Table**

- `stg_transactions`

---

# Business Objectives

- Analyze payment method adoption.
- Compare revenue generated by each payment type.
- Measure customer payment preferences.
- Track payment trends over time.
- Support payment infrastructure planning.
- Identify high-value payment transactions.

# Analysis Performed

## 1. Payment Method Performance

This analysis calculates:

- Total Transactions
- Total Revenue
- Average Transaction Value

Business Value:

Helps identify the most commonly used payment channel.

### Business Insight

Swipe Transactions account for the largest share of transactions, while Online Transactions have the highest average transaction value.

---

## 2. Payment Method Distribution

This calculates the percentage contribution of each payment method.

Business Value:

Measures customer payment preferences.

### Business Insight

More than half of all transactions are completed using Swipe Transactions, followed by Chip Transactions and Online Transactions.

---

## 3. Monthly Payment Trend

Monthly transaction count and revenue are calculated for every payment method.

Business Value:

Shows seasonal trends and payment adoption over time.

### Business Insight

Online Transactions gradually increase over the years, reflecting digital payment adoption.

---

## 4. Transaction Value Comparison

This compares:

- Average Transaction
- Highest Transaction
- Lowest Transaction

for every payment method.

Business Value:

Identifies which payment methods are associated with high-value purchases.

### Business Insight

Online Transactions show the highest average purchase value despite having fewer transactions.

---

## 5. Yearly Payment Method Analysis

Year-wise payment statistics include:

- Total Transactions
- Revenue

Business Value:

Tracks long-term changes in customer payment behavior.

### Business Insight

Payment usage remains stable across years while online payments show gradual growth.

---

## 6. Revenue Contribution

Calculates each payment method's contribution to total business revenue.

Business Value:

Helps prioritize payment infrastructure investments.

### Business Insight

Swipe Transactions contribute approximately half of total revenue, followed by Chip and Online Transactions.

---

## 7. Highest Value Transactions

Displays the largest individual transactions across all payment methods.

Business Value:

Useful for:

- Fraud Detection
- Risk Monitoring
- Transaction Auditing

### Business Insight

Several Online and Swipe Transactions appear among the highest-value purchases, indicating these payment methods support high-ticket transactions.

---

# Key Findings

- Swipe Transactions dominate transaction volume.
- Chip Transactions remain a significant payment channel.
- Online Transactions generate the highest average transaction value.
- Digital payment adoption increases steadily over time.
- Payment revenue is well distributed across multiple payment channels.
- High-value purchases occur across all payment methods.

---
